Cette pièce identifie les **canaux officiels** et fixe le **texte exact** à envoyer.
Elle n'envoie rien. Un agent de développement n'a pas accès à une boîte d'expédition
du projet : l'envoi est une action **humaine**.

Aucune souscription, aucun téléchargement SPY, aucun appel d'API.

Les questions normatives restent celles de l'annexe INQ (français). Les corps
ci-dessous sont la **traduction anglaise fidèle** destinée aux destinataires.
En cas d'écart, l'INQ prime pour la re-notation ; le texte **envoyé** (ci-dessous,
copie exacte) prime pour l'archive de l'échange.

## Corps T — Tiingo

**To:** sales@tiingo.com
**Subject:** Written research questions — Tiingo Power EOD (SPY) — methodology and interface version

```text
Hello,

I am conducting a documented source-selection review for personal research.
I am not requesting a trial, a subscription change, a quote, or any data
download.

Product in scope: Tiingo End-of-Day, US ETF SPY, Power plan.

Please answer each question with a closed, verifiable statement and, if it
exists, a URL or exact clause in your official documentation.

---

Q-T1 — data-source splicing (our criterion PA-11)

Does the published End-of-Day history for US ETFs, including SPY, come from
a single upstream source over the entire price period that Tiingo publishes
for that instrument, or from more than one source joined over time?

If more than one source: which sources, and what are the join dates?

Is this stated on an official page or in a contractual clause? If yes,
please give the exact URL or reference.

---

Q-T2 — interface version (our criterion PA-14)

Does the interface GET /tiingo/daily/<ticker>/prices (and its CSV
equivalent) have a version identifier that a client can record, and that
changes when the schema or the field semantics change?

If yes: what is the current identifier, and where is the versioning policy
published?

---

A marketing statement that you provide adjusted historical data is not
sufficient for these two questions.

I will archive your reply as written evidence. Thank you.

```
